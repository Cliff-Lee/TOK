SL Arc length and sector area in degrees

\[s=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\cdot 2\pi r\]\[A=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\cdot \pi r^2\]

At SL, the central idea is “the same fraction of a full circle”.

Example. A sector has radius \(8\) cm and angle \(135^\circ\). Find its arc length and area.

\[s=\frac{135}{360}\cdot2\pi(8)=6\pi\approx18.85\text{ cm}\]\[A=\frac{135}{360}\cdot\pi(8^2)=24\pi\approx75.40\text{ cm}^2\]

HL What is a radian?

An angle is \(1\) radian when the arc length it cuts off is exactly equal to the radius.

r = 6

HL Radian meaning

\[\theta=\frac{s}{r}\]\[1\text{ radian means }s=r\]
Radians are dimensionless because they compare one length to another length.

HL Convert degrees and radians

\[\text{radians}=\text{degrees}\times\frac{\pi}{180^\circ}\]\[\text{degrees}=\text{radians}\times\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\]

The anchor fact is \(180^\circ=\pi\) radians.
