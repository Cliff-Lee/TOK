What did the robot discover?

Hint 1: Coordinates must match

Each button changes x, y, and z at the same time. To land on the treasure, all three coordinates must match the target together.

Hint 2: Two vectors in 3D make a plane

If u and v are not parallel, their combinations make a flat sheet through base camp. A target outside that sheet cannot be reached, even with decimal button presses.

Hint 3: Whole numbers are stricter

A decimal solution means the arrows point to the right place, but the robot still fails the mission if it needs half a button press.
