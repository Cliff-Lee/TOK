1. Understand the problem What is fixed? What changes?
  • What is the original chord?
  • Which point is its midpoint?
  • Which two chords pass through the midpoint?
  • Which two new points are created on the original chord?
2. Make a plan What evidence will you collect?
  • Change the original chord position.
  • Change the two crossing chords.
  • Measure the two green segments on the original chord.
  • Look for a relationship that stays true in different diagrams.
