Use Polya's Method

No solution shown
1. Understand the problem What is being constructed?
  • What is the starting shape?
  • What is built on each side?
  • Which centres are connected?
  • What does it mean for two segments to be related?
2. Make a plan What evidence will you collect?
  • Move the four vertices to create different quadrilaterals.
  • Record the two segment lengths and the angle between them.
  • Look for measurements that stay the same or nearly the same.
  • Decide which examples would be most useful to test your conjecture.

4. Look back and justify Why might it always work?
  • What does constructing a square guarantee about lengths and angles?
  • Can you use a rotation of 90 degrees to compare parts of the diagram?
  • Can you draw extra lines between original vertices and square centres?
  • How could you convince someone without using more measurements?
Extension question: What changes if the squares are built inward instead of outward? Test several cases before making a new conjecture.
