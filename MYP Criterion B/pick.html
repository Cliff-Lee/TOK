1. Understand the problem What is being counted?
  • What makes a point a lattice point?
  • Which lattice points are on the boundary?
  • Which lattice points are inside the polygon?
  • How can you find the area of the polygon?
2. Make a plan How will you collect evidence?
  • Start with simple polygons, such as rectangles and right triangles.
  • Record the area, boundary points, and interior points.
  • Then test slanted and concave polygons.
  • Look for a relationship that works for all valid examples.

4. Look back and justify Why might it always work?
