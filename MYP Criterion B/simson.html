Use Polya's Method

No solution shown
1. Understand the problem What is being measured?
  • What is the starting triangle?
  • What is special about point P?
  • What does it mean to drop a perpendicular to a side?
  • What would show that three points are on one straight line?
2. Make a plan How will you test different cases?
  • Move P to different places on the circumcircle.
  • Try several different triangles.
  • Record a measurement that tests whether the three feet are collinear.
  • Choose examples that could break your conjecture if it is wrong.

4. Look back and justify Why might it always work?
  • Can you find equal or supplementary angles on the circumcircle?
  • Can you find right angles made by the perpendiculars?
  • Can you use angle chasing to compare the directions of the small segments?
  • How could you convince someone without using more measurements?
Extension question: What happens if P is moved off the circumcircle? Make a prediction, then test it by sketching your own example.
