Right-trig idea

When there is a right angle, use side ratios relative to a chosen angle.

SOHCAHTOA
SOH\(\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\)
CAH\(\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}\)
TOA\(\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}\)

Non-right triangle idea

When there is no right angle, you often create one by dropping a perpendicular height.

\(\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}\)

Correction idea

The cosine rule is Pythagoras with an angle correction.

\(a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A\)

Why students often choose the wrong rule

They look for numbers rather than relationships. A better habit is to circle any side-angle opposite pair and mark whether an angle is included between two known sides.

HL extension: ambiguous case of the sine rule

This is the SSA situation: two sides and a non-included angle. It can make zero, one, or two possible triangles.

HL calculation guide

The calculator gives one inverse-sine angle, but sine has the same value for two angles in \(0^\circ\) to \(180^\circ\).

\[ \frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \sin B=\frac{b\sin A}{a}. \]
No triangle

\(a<h\)

The side \(a\) is too short to reach the baseline. The circle misses the line.

One triangle

\(a=h\)

The side \(a\) just touches the baseline. This gives one right triangle.

Two triangles

\(h<a<b\)

The circle cuts the baseline twice, giving two valid positions for \(B\).

One triangle

\(a\ge b\)

The second possible angle would make the triangle angle sum exceed \(180^\circ\).

Measurement discussion

Real trigonometry includes uncertainty. Ask: How accurate is the angle measurement? Is the ground level? Did you measure to eye level or base level? Which length is directly measured and which is calculated?

Design principles used

visual first proof optional feedback after choice diagram-driven

This applet intentionally separates method choice from calculation. Students often know formulas but fail to recognise when to use them. The key repeated question is: “What information do we have, and what relationship does the diagram show?”

How to use on a website

Save this file as index.html and upload it to Netlify, Vercel, or any static web host. It is a single-file applet. Math typesetting uses MathJax from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed for the best formula rendering.

Quick classroom task
  1. Students answer five “Choose the rule” questions without calculators.
  2. Students open one proof and write the missing algebra step.
  3. Students solve one real measurement problem and list two possible measurement errors.
  4. Students complete one exam-style problem and mark their own work with the shown solution.
