Key idea

On the unit circle, tan(θ) is the height where the angle ray reaches x = 1. On the graph, each angle θ is plotted against that height.

angle θ → horizontal position tan(θ) → vertical height 90° and 270° → asymptotes period = π

How sin, cos, and tan are linked

Since tan(x) = sin(x) / cos(x), the zeroes of sin become zeroes of tan, and the zeroes of cos become vertical asymptotes of tan.

cos(x) = 0 tan(x) = sin(x) / cos(x) has denominator 0. tan has a vertical asymptote at x = π/2 + kπ.

Key connection

The tangent graph is not separate from sine and cosine. It is their ratio. Zero numerator gives a zero. Zero denominator gives an asymptote.

sin zero → tan zero cos zero → tan asymptote same x-scale tan = sin ÷ cos
